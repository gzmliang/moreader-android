# Moreader — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 13, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Moreader ("we", "our", or "the App") is an EPUB reader and text-to-speech companion designed for readers and language learners. We operate under a strict **local-first, zero-data-collection** architecture.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical locations.
- **No Account Registration:** You can use all core reading features without registering or creating an account.
- **No Reading History Tracking:** Your books, highlights, reading progress, and bookmarks remain entirely on your local device or your designated personal WebDAV server. We operate no tracking servers.
- **No Third-Party Trackers or Ads:** We do not employ third-party behavioral trackers, marketing SDKs, or advertising networks.

## 3. Permissions & Local Data Usage
- **INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE:** Required solely to stream text-to-speech neural audio from edge services or to communicate with AI endpoints and WebDAV servers explicitly configured by the user.

## 4. Third-Party AI Services (Optional)
If you choose to configure and use optional AI reading features (such as character relationship extraction or chapter summaries), your selected text is transmitted directly from your device to the API provider of your choice (e.g., OpenAI, DeepSeek, or your private endpoint). Moreader does not intercept, log, or resell your prompts.
